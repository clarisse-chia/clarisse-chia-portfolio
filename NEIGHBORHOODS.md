# Ten neighborhoods for The Underground

This is a curated selection for a small portfolio experience, not a ranking by ridership. Together the clusters represent all 22 non-shuttle subway route letters/numbers across Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, and the Bronx.

## Selection rules

- Start with a recognizable neighborhood and a named anchor station.
- Include useful subway stations roughly within a ten-minute street walk of that anchor. A cluster does not promise a ten-minute walk between every possible pair of member stations.
- Prefer a compact, understandable area over adding more route badges.
- Preserve a clear boarding location on every arrival. Neighborhood membership does not imply a free transfer or a connected station complex.
- Keep walking estimates separate from train ETAs. These are editorial estimates, not measured walking routes, accessible routing, or directions from the visitor's location.

## Scope choices

Williamsburg stays centered on Lorimer / Metropolitan rather than stretching west to Bedford. Harlem excludes the farther Lexington Avenue 4/5/6 station. The Bronx cluster stays within the Yankee Stadium complex rather than forcing an extra 167 St station into the walking boundary.

Long Island City's separate Queens Plaza and Queensboro Plaza stations are named individually. Current official route maps show F at Court Sq and Queens Plaza; M uses the other Manhattan–Queens crossing. Service can change by time, day, and planned work. Z and some other services have limited operating periods; route inclusion does not promise a prediction at every moment.

All displayed ETAs belong to a named member station. The same train can appear at two stations, with a distinct ETA for each. Visitors can choose a station with “Board at,” filter a route, or inspect the complete station list.

No S shuttles, Staten Island Railway, PATH, LIRR, Metro-North, buses, or ferries are included. The map is an original schematic with simplified positions, not a full routing map.
